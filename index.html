WhatsApp Agents

10 WhatsApp business agents in one skill: receptionist, lead qualifier, appointments, sales, support, follow-up, personal shopper, feedback, internal knowledge and CRM logger. Paste your business profile once, then any incoming message, and get a short reply plus a CRM line. It never invents prices, times or policies. Made by AI Walkthru (MIT).

Download SKILL.md
  1. Tap Download SKILL.md above (on a laptop).
  2. In Claude, click Customize in the left sidebar.
  3. Skills → + Add (top right) → Upload skill.
  4. Choose SKILL.md, check the preview, then Upload.
  5. In a new chat, type /whatsapp-agents, paste your business profile and the customer's message, then send.
It drafts replies in Claude. To auto-send on WhatsApp you also need the WhatsApp Business Platform and an automation tool; keep a human able to take over.

Humanizer

Makes AI-sounding writing read like a person wrote it. Built from Wikipedia's guide to spotting AI writing; it checks 25 habits and never invents facts. Skill by blader (MIT).

Watch the full tutorial Get SKILL.md on GitHub
  1. On the GitHub page, click the download arrow next to Raw. Skip "Download ZIP".
  2. In Claude, click Customize in the left sidebar.
  3. Skills → + Add (top right) → Upload skill.
  4. Click the upload box, pick SKILL.md from Downloads, then Upload.
  5. In a new chat, type /humanizer, paste your text and send.
Do this on a laptop or desktop at claude.ai. Code execution must be switched on in Settings.

Content Team

Eight AI specialists inside Claude (strategist, trend researcher, script writer, editor, thumbnail designer, SEO, social media, community) run with one command.

Download content-team.plugin Full guide with screenshots
  1. Download the .plugin file above.
  2. In Claude, click Customize in the left sidebar.
  3. Plugins → + Add → Upload plugin.
  4. Drop in the file, then Upload.
  5. Type /content-team and your topic.

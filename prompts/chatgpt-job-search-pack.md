# Automate your job search with ChatGPT: the test pack

From the AI Walkthru video "I Let ChatGPT Run My Job Search" (tested 17 November 2026 on ChatGPT Plus, one run). Swap in your own CV and rules.

## 1. The rules file (save as job-rules.md, upload to the project's Sources with your CV)

### My job search rules

- Role: Customer Success Manager (not Senior, Principal, Enterprise, Strategic, or a manager of a team).
- Where: remote in the US (Colorado must be allowed), or in Denver, Colorado.
- Company: a software (SaaS) company.
- Pay: at least $75,000 base, if the posting shows pay.
- Only jobs that are open today, with a link to the company's own job page.
- Never invent experience. Use only what is in my CV.

## 2. Project instructions (Project settings > Instructions)

You are my job search assistant. My CV and my rules are in this project's sources. Follow job-rules.md every time. Use only facts from my CV and never invent experience, numbers or skills. Always link to the company's own job page.

## 3. The prompts, in order

**Chat mode, in the project**

1. Read my CV and my rules. Make my job search plan: the job titles to search, the keywords recruiters use, ten software companies that hire this role in Denver or remote, and a simple weekly routine. Keep it short.

2. (Turn on Web search from the + menu) Search the web for Customer Success Manager jobs that are open today and match every rule in job-rules.md. Show up to 10 in a table: company, title, location, pay, and a link to the company's own job page. Leave out any job that breaks a rule.

3. Only 3? Search wider. Check the careers pages of more SaaS companies, including jobs on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards. Add only new jobs that match every rule, in the same table.

**Work mode, project chosen** (Work can't read another chat's tables: press Copy under each answer and paste them in first)

4. Read the chat "Create Job Search Plan" in this project. Open every job link from its tables in your browser. Check that each job is still open and still matches my rules. Then make me a job tracker spreadsheet with these columns: company, title, location, pay, link, status, date checked, next step.

5. Pick the best match for me from the tracker and tell me why in two lines. Then make a tailored copy of my CV for that job and a short cover letter, both as Word documents. Use only facts from my CV, and list every change you made.

6. Open the Motus application page in your browser and fill in the form with my details from my CV. Attach my tailored CV and cover letter. Leave any question about salary, visa or demographics empty and tell me about it. Do not press Submit. Stop and show me the filled form.

**Chat mode, in the project**

7. Every weekday at 8 AM, search for new Customer Success Manager jobs that match my rules and are not already in my tracker. Check each link is open, then send me only the new ones with their links.

## 4. Check before you apply

- Open every job link yourself: in my run 1 of 13 was dead.
- Search the big job boards too: ChatGPT found 1 of the 6 matching jobs in my answer key.
- Read the CV and letter against your real CV, line by line.
- Attach your files, answer the personal questions and press Submit yourself. Indeed and LinkedIn ban bots that apply for you.

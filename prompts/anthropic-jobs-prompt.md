# How exposed is my job to AI? The prompt we tested

From the AI Walkthru carousel "Junior tech hires at the AI lab: 27% → 9%" (tested in claude.ai, 19 October 2026, Opus 5.5).

The numbers come from Anthropic's own report, "How employment is changing at Anthropic" (16 October 2026): anthropic.com/research/employment-at-anthropic

## How to use it

1. Open **claude.ai** (a free account works; an incognito chat keeps it out of your history).
2. Copy the prompt below and paste it in.
3. Replace the two bracketed parts with your job title and one line about what you do each day. The more specific the line, the sharper the table.
4. Send it. Our answers took about 21 seconds, and Claude searched the web to check the figures first.

## The prompt

```
Anthropic just published data on how AI changed its own workforce (Oct 16, 2026):
- Junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9%.
- AI agent hours now outnumber employee hours 6 to 1.
- "Meta-work" (managing and checking AI agents) rose from 10% to 22% of people's AI sessions.
- Hiring managers now focus on autonomy, judgment and creative use of AI tools, and less on narrow domain expertise.

My job: [Your job title]. [One line on what you do each day.]

Using that data:
1. Split my job into its main tasks in a table. Mark each one: AI does it now / AI helps / stays human.
2. Give my role an AI-exposure score out of 10, with one sentence why.
3. Name the 3 skills I should build in the next 90 days, with one concrete action each.
4. Write one resume line that proves "creative use of AI tools".
Keep it under 250 words.
```

## What we got (same prompt, four jobs)

| Job | Exposure score Claude gave |
|---|---|
| Junior software developer | 8/10 |
| Executive assistant | 7/10 |
| Marketing coordinator | 8/10 |
| Registered nurse on a hospital ward | 3/10 |

Each answer also gave a task table (AI does it now / AI helps / stays human), three skills to build in 90 days with one action each, and a resume line.

## Read this before you act on it

- The scores are Claude's judgment, not Anthropic's.
- Anthropic's data covers one AI company, and the junior-hire drop is for technical roles. Claude pointed this out itself: treat it as a signal, not a forecast.
- The resume lines come with example numbers. Claude said so in every run: replace them with your real results.

## What the report says hiring managers look for now

- Creative use of AI tools: mentioned three times as often for technical roles, and four times as often for non-technical roles, as in 2024.
- Autonomy and judgment: the biggest increase in technical interviews.
- Customer and mission focus: rising for non-technical roles.
- Narrow domain expertise and execution skills: a shrinking share of the feedback.

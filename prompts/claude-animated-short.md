# Make a viral animated Short in one Claude chat (free prompts)

From the AI Walkthru video "I Made a Viral Animated Short in ONE Claude Chat". Tested on claude.ai with Claude Fable 5.2 at High effort, 12 November 2026. Paste them in order, in ONE chat. Change the story in prompt 2 to your own.

## 1. The formula

I want to make viral animated YouTube Shorts in the style where flat 2D cartoon characters stand inside a real 3D world, like the history explainers that get millions of views. Search the web and give me the formula on one page: how the script is built line by line, and a rule book for the look (light, colour, outlines, camera, cuts, pace).

## 2. The script (change the story)

Use that formula to write the script for a 60-second Short about the 1904 Olympic marathon. Check every fact with a web search, write one short line per shot, and list your sources under the script.

## 3. The build

Now build the Short as one HTML artifact I can export. Follow your rule book for the look: a three.js 3D world with black ink outlines, flat 2D characters that catch the light and cast shadows, a camera that glides and almost never cuts, the light turning night blue at "You see," and bold captions on each word. Use your script line by line. Under the video add Play, a Voice file button that fits the shots to my recording, and Record, which saves a 1080x1920 video with my voice in it.

## 4. The fix (write what you saw)

Watch your Short, pause on a few frames and compare them with the rule book from prompt 1. Then name the faults in numbered points, for example:

I watched it against your rule book. Three fixes: 1) Most 3D objects have no ink lines (the apple tree, the wagon, the rule book box): trace every silhouette and crease with about 3 px of near-black ink. 2) Long caption words are cut off at the edge, like FAVORITE: shrink any word that does not fit. 3) The colours look dull: push the saturation and lift the darks as your rule book says. Keep everything else the same.

## Voice and export

1. Record yourself reading the script on your phone. Leave a small breath between lines: the page listens for pauses.
2. In the artifact, press Voice file and pick your recording. The shots and captions are retimed to it.
3. Press Record. It renders every frame at 1080x1920 with your voice in it.
4. Press Save video, then Save file when claude.ai asks.
5. No music is added: put a track under it in any editor (CapCut, iMovie).

## What to check

- The first line is a paradox; "You see," comes once; the last line is a twist.
- Every fact has a source. Claude flags lines the sources disagree on: keep its cautions.
- Ink lines on the 3D objects, captions that fit, strong colours. If not, use prompt 4.
- Around a fast read the shots can land up to a second early: use the 'Start 0.1 s earlier / later' buttons.

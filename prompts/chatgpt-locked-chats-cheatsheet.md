# ChatGPT Locked Chats: cheat sheet

From AI Walkthru (@aiwalkthru). Source: OpenAI Help Center, "Using locked chats in ChatGPT" (Oct 20, 2026), and TechCrunch (Oct 20, 2026).

## Is it on your account?

Locked chats are rolling out gradually. Open any chat and tap the ••• menu. If you don't see **Lock chat**, it isn't on your account yet. On Oct 20, 2026 our ChatGPT Plus account did not have it.

Plans: Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu. Not ChatGPT for Healthcare. On a computer, use the ChatGPT website or the new ChatGPT app for macOS or Windows.

## Lock a chat

1. Open the chat's ••• menu.
2. Select **Lock chat**.
3. Select **Lock chat** again to confirm.
4. Create a 4-digit PIN and enter it again to confirm (only the first time).

Find locked chats under **Projects → Locked chats**. Enter your PIN, or use Face ID, Touch ID or your phone unlock on supported phones.

## What it hides

- Regular chat history
- Pinned chats
- Regular chat search
- Chat titles in the sidebar, until you enter your PIN

## Catch 1: a lock, not a vault

Locking a chat does not:

- add end-to-end encryption or a separate encrypted vault
- change how long OpenAI keeps the chat
- change your "Improve the model for everyone" setting
- remove what ChatGPT remembered before you locked it
- override workspace policies or legal obligations

Anyone who can open a locked chat can still copy its contents.

## Catch 2: the PIN trap

- **Changing** your PIN (Settings → Security and login → Account PIN → Change) keeps your locked chats.
- **Resetting** a forgotten PIN permanently deletes every locked chat, memories stored only in locked chats, and files protected only by the old PIN. OpenAI Support cannot recover them, and a workspace admin cannot reset your PIN.
- Save your PIN in a password manager before you lock anything.

## Catch 3: memory flows one way

- Locked chats can use your regular Memory.
- Locked chats share their own Memory with each other.
- What is remembered inside locked chats never carries into regular chats.
- If you permanently unlock a chat, it returns to your history and may be used for regular Memory. Memories made while it was locked don't come along.

## Catch 4: it re-locks itself

- **Web and desktop:** access ends when you refresh, close the tab or step away from it. A new tab asks for the PIN again.
- **iOS and Android:** access ends after 1 minute away from locked chats or 1 minute in the background.
- Select **Lock now** to hide titles right away.

## What can't be locked

Temporary chats, archived chats, group chats, chats created from a shared link, chats with scheduled tasks, chats with meeting recordings, and Codex conversations. A locked chat can't be pinned, moved, shared or archived until you permanently unlock it.

## Locked chat vs temporary chat

- Temporary chat: never appears in history, and you can't come back to it.
- Locked chat: hidden from history, and you can come back any time with your PIN.

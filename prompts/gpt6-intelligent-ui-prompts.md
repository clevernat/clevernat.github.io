# 6 prompts to test ChatGPT's new GPT-6 answers

From the AI Walkthru carousel "ChatGPT stopped answering in text" (tested on ChatGPT Plus, 17 October 2026).

## Turn it on first

1. Open ChatGPT and tap the model button next to Send.
2. Tap the version name.
3. Pick **GPT-6**. Our account was still set to GPT-5.6, so check yours.

GPT-6 with Intelligent UI reached Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise on 7 October 2026 (GPT-6 Sol) and Free and Go from 8 October (GPT-6 Luna), according to OpenAI.

## The prompts and what we got

**1. Road trip (map, photos, form)**
> Plan a 3-day road trip from Denver to Moab. Show me the stops worth a detour.

We got a live map with 10 numbered stops, 14 photos and a day-by-day schedule. On one run it also added a form: pick "Round trip" and "More hiking", tap Customize, and it rebuilt the whole plan in 23 seconds. Swap in your own cities.

**2. Rent or buy (slider)**
> Should I rent or buy? I pay $1,800 a month in rent and the house I like costs $350,000.

We got a down-payment slider. At 5% down it showed $1,344 more a month than rent; at 30% down, $588 more. We checked the maths by hand. Use your own rent and price.

**3. Compound interest (chart)**
> Explain how compound interest works. I'm 25 and can save $200 a month.

We got a chart and a table ($524,963 at 65, at an illustrative 7% a year). It also promised an "interactive example" that never appeared, so expect the odd slip.

**4. Split a bill (plain answer)**
> Help me split a $186 dinner bill between 5 friends with an 18% tip.

A plain table in 8 seconds: $43.90 each. Simple questions stay simple.

**5. Quick fact (plain answer)**
> What's the capital of Australia?

One line in 6 seconds: Canberra.

**6. Ask for the tool (calculator)**
> Make me a tip calculator I can use at dinner tonight.

A working calculator with a bill box, tip buttons and a split counter. $186, 18% and 5 people gave $43.90 each, the same as prompt 4.

## Pro tip

If you want an app, ask for one: "make me a ___ calculator", "show me on a map", "let me compare with a slider".

Estimates in these answers (mortgage, investment growth, drive times) are illustrative. Check real numbers before you decide anything.

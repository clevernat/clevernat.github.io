# 12 prompts to test ChatGPT's new GPT-6 answers

From the AI Walkthru video "ChatGPT Stopped Answering in Text: GPT-6 Tested on 12 Real Prompts" (tested on ChatGPT Plus, 18 October 2026). Prompts 1 to 6 were also in the carousel.

## Turn it on first

1. Open ChatGPT and tap the model button next to Send.
2. Stay in the **Chat** tab and pick **GPT-6**. Our account was still set to GPT-5.6, so check yours.

GPT-6 with Intelligent UI reached Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise on 7 October 2026 (GPT-6 Sol) and Free and Go from 8 October (GPT-6 Luna), according to OpenAI.

Answers change from run to run. The results below are from the run in the video.

## The prompts and what we got

**1. Road trip (live map, photos)**
> Plan a 3-day road trip from Denver to Moab. Show me the stops worth a detour.

A live map with every stop pinned, real photos and a day-by-day plan. Follow-up that rebuilt the plan: "Round trip, back in Denver by the end of Day 3."

**2. Rent or buy (sliders and a form)**
> Should I rent or buy? I pay $1,800 a month in rent and the house I like costs $350,000.

Two live sliders. At 20% down: $2,615 a month. At 5% down: $2,978. At 40% down with a 5.6% rate: $1,882. The form at the bottom (10% down, 5 to 7 years) built a new report: $2,988 a month with mortgage insurance.

**3. Compound interest (the slip)**
> Explain how compound interest works. I'm 25 and can save $200 a month.

A chart and a table ($524,963 at 65, at an illustrative 7% a year). It promised an interactive illustration that never appeared. When we asked for it, it built a calculator with three sliders; at 9% the balance went to $936,264.

> You said there's an interactive illustration where I can change the interest rate and the number of years, but I don't see it. Can you show it?

**4. Split a bill (plain answer)**
> Help me split a $186 dinner bill between 5 friends with an 18% tip.

A plain table: $43.90 each. Simple questions stay simple.

**5. Quick fact (plain answer)**
> What's the capital of Australia?

One line: Canberra.

**6. Ask for the tool (calculator)**
> Make me a tip calculator I can use at dinner tonight.

A working calculator. $240, 20% and 4 people gave $72.00 each.

**7. Planet quiz**
> Quiz me on the planets of the solar system. Make it fun.

A five-question quiz with instant feedback. We scored 5 out of 5.

**8. How the heart works (interactive)**
> Show me how the heart pumps blood. Make it interactive.

A beating heart you can step through, with a heart-rate slider.

**9. Meal plan on a budget**
> Plan my meals for the week. I have a $75 grocery budget.

A 7-day plan at $68.50 with a grocery list you can tick off and a running total. The form at the bottom (vegetarian, higher protein) rebuilt it at $71.56.

**10. Paint colors**
> Help me choose a paint color for a small bedroom that doesn't get much light.

Four real colors shown in real bedrooms. After we picked "warm, cozy and relaxing", its pick was Pale Oak.

**11. Flashcards**
> Make me flashcards to learn 10 basic Spanish words.

A deck of 10 cards you flip, with pronunciation, and tick or cross to track your progress.

**12. Car loan (sliders)**
> How does a car loan work? Show me for a $25,000 car at 6% over 5 years.

$483.32 a month and $3,999.20 in interest. Sliders for down payment, rate and term: $5,000 down gave $386.66 a month; 3 years gave $608.44 a month and $1,904 in interest. We checked every figure with the loan formula.

## Pro tip

If you want an app, ask for one: "make me a ___ calculator", "show me on a map", "let me compare with a slider".

Estimates in these answers (mortgage, investment growth, loan costs, grocery prices) are illustrative. Check real numbers before you decide anything.

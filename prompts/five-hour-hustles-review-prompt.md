# The Claude review message (+ the 8 steps I used for every order)

From the AI Walkthru video "Claude Side Hustles for People Who Only Have 5 Hours a Week (I Timed Every One)".
Free to use. No income promises: these are test orders for made-up clients, and finding real clients is up to you.

## The review message

Send this as a follow-up, word for word, after Claude delivers the first version:

```
Before I send this to the client, check your work against their order line by line. List anything you added that was not in their files, any limit or rule you broke, and anything you left out. Then give me the corrected final version as a downloadable file.
```

## The 8 steps

1. Open claude.ai, start a new chat and click the ghost icon (incognito), so your saved memory doesn't change the result.
2. Click the + button, choose Add files, pick the client's file(s), click Open.
3. Paste the client's order message exactly as they sent it, and add: `Deliver it as a downloadable file.`
4. Click send and wait.
5. Read the reply, then click Download to save the first draft.
6. Paste the review message above and send it.
7. Download the corrected file.
8. Read every word yourself before it goes to the client. In my tests the review fixed more than it broke, but not always (on the spreadsheet order it made things worse).

## Rules that kept my scores high

- Never let a number go out that the client hasn't confirmed or that their own documents don't prove.
- Real-estate listings: describe the home, never who should live in it (Fair Housing). The agent is responsible for the final text.
- Review replies: the owner approves every reply before it is posted.

# Gemini Notebook study tools: the exact steps and prompts we tested

From the AI Walkthru carousel "3 new study tools. I tested all 3." (tested on a Google AI Pro account, 18 October 2026, with OpenStax *Biology 2e* chapters 8.1–8.3 as the notes).

Google announced these on 16 October 2026 (blog.google, "3 tips to study smarter with Gemini Notebook").

## Step 1: add your notes

1. Open **notebook.google.com** and tap **New notebook**.
2. Tap **Add sources**, then **Websites**, and paste your course links (or upload your PDFs and slides).
3. A source that says "URL not found" failed to load. Ours was a typo in the link: fix it and add it again.

## Tool 1: Short Video Overview

1. In **Studio**, tap the **›** arrow on **Video Overview**.
2. Under Format, pick **Short**.
3. In **Custom topic**, name the one idea you find hard, then tap **Generate now**.

Our topic:
> Explain how the Calvin cycle turns CO2 into sugar

What we got: a 1 minute 19 second vertical video, hand-drawn style, that matched the textbook. It took about 9½ minutes to make. Google calls it a 60-second video.

## Tool 2: Interactive Learning Overview

1. In **Studio**, tap the **›** arrow on **Reports**.
2. Pick **Interactive**, keep the **Learning Overview** template, and tap **Generate now**.

What we got: a 6-section lesson in under 40 seconds, with facts taken from the chapters. Slides, quizzes, flashcards, a mind map and an infographic were *suggested* inside it: tap **Add** on one to make it. Our flashcards came back as 25 cards in 35 seconds.

## Tool 3: quizzes and feedback

1. In **Studio**, tap the **›** arrow on **Quiz** and tap **Generate now**.
2. Take the quiz. The score screen lists what you missed.
3. Then ask the notebook chat:

> I just took the Photosynthesis Quiz. Based on my results, where should I focus next?

What we got: it quoted our score (8 of 12) and reviewed exactly the 4 topics we missed, with citations, in 18 seconds.

**The new quiz formats** (short answer, multi-select, fill in the blank) did not appear in the Quiz tool for us: all 12 questions were multiple choice, and the Quiz menu has no format option. The chat will write them as plain text:

> Quiz me with a short-answer question, a multi-select question and a fill-in-the-blank question on the Calvin cycle.

## Pro tip

After any quiz or flashcard round, ask "Based on my results, where should I focus next?" It turns your mistakes into a study plan.
